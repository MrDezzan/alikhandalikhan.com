Qozhal Tracker

by Alikhan Dalikhan

Cameras and computer vision that watch cattle around the clock and tell the farmer when something is off.

Qozhal Tracker is an AgriTech product. Cameras on the farm feed a computer vision system that follows the behavior of each animal 24/7: how much it moves, when it feeds and when it drinks. If an animal eats or moves noticeably less than it should, the farmer gets an alert.

A pilot farm is running with the system. I'm the founder and CEO and I handle all the technical work. My co-founder handles marketing.

In 2026 Qozhal Tracker took 2nd place at Talent Pitch, part of the international exhibition Digital Bridge 2026. The prize includes an internship, funding and mentorship.
