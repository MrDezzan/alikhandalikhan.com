About

I'm Alikhan Dalikhan, a high-school student in Astana, Kazakhstan. I build software that has to work in the real world: computer vision that monitors cattle on a pilot farm, an AI platform that helps students pick a profession, and an app for people who are afraid to speak in public.

In my teams I take the technical side. For Qozhal Tracker that is everything technical, and in OratoAI I built the frontend and the AI part. I also lead people: I co-led a team of 500 volunteers at Jas Volunteers and coordinated 50 volunteers at a safety-day event.

I plan to study Computer Science, then continue in AI engineering or cybersecurity.
